About Musafir Cafe

A Journey in Every Cup

Musafir Café is a cozy hill-station café created for travellers, dreamers, and coffee lovers.

The word “Musafir” means Traveller — someone who enjoys the journey as much as the destination. Inspired by the beauty of the mountains, our café is a peaceful place to slow down, enjoy freshly brewed coffee, taste delicious food, and create unforgettable memories. Whether you are watching the sunrise, enjoying a sunset with friends, or simply sitting quietly with a cup of coffee, Musafir Café gives every traveller a place to pause and enjoy the moment. Come for the mountains. Stay for the coffee. Leave with a story.
